About

Two kinds of filter: Topics say what a paper is about (from its title and abstract, any outlet); Outlets say where it was published.

Every week this digest scans new articles in the top‑5 economics journals, theory journals (TE, JET, GEB…), general-interest journals (JEEA, EJ, REStat, IER, AER: Insights), field journals (RAND, JIE, AEJs, economic history), management & strategy journals, philosophy & psychology, statistics & applied math, broad science outlets, and arXiv (econ.TH, cs.GT, econ.GN).

Papers are tagged and ranked for: game & economic theory, innovation & entrepreneurship, industrial organization & AI; and, secondarily, history, decision theory, technology/energy/robotics, and physics & biology.

Search syntax: AND, OR, NOT, parentheses and "quoted phrases". Adjacent words imply AND. Prefix a word with author:, title: or journal: to search one field.
